Approach

What I bring to a finance team.

My work sits at the intersection of FP&A, M&A, and investment management — I read a business from the top down, at the level of macroeconomic structure and market share, and from the bottom up, at the level of a three-statement model and a single line of capex. That range is what lets me move between very different asset types: technology companies where I'm underwriting long-range revenue against operational profitability, and real estate portfolios where the same discipline gets applied to occupancy rates, DSCR, and land valuation.

I've built models on companies ranging from early-stage AI labs to public technology leaders, and before that, co-founded and helped run a private real estate investment partnership from the ground up — sourcing deals, underwriting risk, and living with the outcomes of my own analysis. That combination means I don't just build a model to spec; I understand what the output is actually being used to decide, and I build accordingly.

What I'd bring to a finance team: a modeler who's comfortable owning a deal end-to-end, fluent across DCF, LBO, and accretion/dilution frameworks, and grounded enough in macro context to know when the assumptions in a spreadsheet stop matching the world outside it.
